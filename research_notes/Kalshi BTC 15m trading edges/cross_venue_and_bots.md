# Cross-venue arbitrage and documented bots on 15-minute BTC up/down markets (Kalshi KXBTC15M, Polymarket, Polymarket US)

Research date: 2026-10-12. Some primary data below comes from my own queries to Polymarket's public Gamma API (`gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=btc-updown-15m-<unix_ts>`), run on 2026-10-12. The URLs are given so they can be reproduced. Several sites (docs.polymarket.com via WebFetch, Yahoo, dev.to, Substack) failed in WebFetch with a DNS error. Those pages were fetched with curl instead, and the content is real.

## 1. Polymarket 15-minute BTC up/down: resolution source, window, fees, liquidity, US access, and how it differs from Kalshi

### Takeaway
There are now **two different Polymarket 15-minute BTC products**, and they settle on different indices:
- **Polymarket international (polymarket.com).** Settles on a **Chainlink BTC/USD 60-second TWAP data stream**. It used a single Chainlink BTC/USD snapshot until early August 2026. US users are geo-restricted.
- **Polymarket US (QCX LLC, a CFTC-regulated DCM).** Launched "BTC Up or Down: 15 min" on about 2026-09-22. It settles on **CF Benchmarks BRTI, using a 60-observation final-minute average at both open and close**, which is effectively the same reference as Kalshi KXBTC15M.

Polymarket international charges a taker fee of `0.07 × C × p × (1−p)` on crypto markets, which peaks at 1.75¢ per share at p=0.50. Makers pay nothing and receive a 20% rebate share.

### Cited Findings
**Resolution source and window (international):**
- Current market text for the BTC 15m market for Oct 11, 10:00–10:15 PM ET: "This market will resolve to 'Up' if the time-weighted average price (TWAP) of Bitcoin, generated by Chainlink, of the time range specified in the title is greater than or equal to the price at the beginning of that range… specifically the BTC/USD TWAP data stream available at https://data.chain.link/streams/btc-usd-twap-60s-streams… not according to any other sources or spot markets." The API fields were `resolutionSource=…btc-usd-twap-60s-streams`, `feesEnabled=True`, `feeType=crypto_fees_v2` and `restricted=True`. — [Polymarket Gamma API, event btc-updown-15m-1791770400](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=btc-updown-15m-1791770400)
- Earlier markets read differently, e.g. Jan 15, 2026 and Mar 31, 2026: "resolve to 'Up' if the Bitcoin price at the end of the time range… is greater than or equal to the price at the beginning… Chainlink… BTC/USD data stream https://data.chain.link/streams/btc-usd". That is a point-in-time snapshot. The Jan 2026 market had `feeType=crypto_15_min`. — [Gamma API, Jan 15 2026 market](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=btc-updown-15m-1768500000) (slug timestamp approximate; see method above)
- I bisected the switch with the API. The Aug 6, 2026 7:30–7:45 PM ET market still used `btc-usd` (snapshot). The Aug 6, 8:00–8:15 PM ET market (00:00 UTC Aug 7) used `btc-usd-twap-60s-streams`. — [Gamma API queries](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=btc-updown-15m-1786060800)
- This matches press reporting that TWAP settlement started **Aug 7, 2026 00:00 UTC**, with a 30s window for 5-minute contracts and 60s for 15-minute and 4-hour contracts. — [Casatrick Substack, Aug 3 2026](https://casatrick.substack.com/p/polymarket-twap-latency-trading-bots); [CryptoTimes, Jul 30 2026](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/30/polymarket-upgrades-crypto-markets-with-twap-pricing-model/); [Genfinity, Aug 12 2026](https://genfinity.io/2026/08/12/chainlink-twap-data-streams-polymarket-crypto-markets/)
- Sources conflict on the 5-minute window. One says 30s at launch, moving to 60s on Aug 14. A Polymarket-focused developer says 60s for the "current 5m / 15m / 4h" markets. — [search summary of TradoxVPS](https://tradoxvps.com/polymarket-twap-settlement/); [dev.to abrownfox001, Aug 28 2026](https://dev.to/abrownfox001/polymarket-twap60-vs-kalshi-why-settlement-design-decides-your-bot-series-14-4gl3)
- TradoxVPS says Chainlink has not published how it computes the TWAP, so outsiders cannot reproduce it exactly. — [TradoxVPS](https://tradoxvps.com/polymarket-twap-settlement/) (via search snippet, not verified directly)
- The open reference ("price at the beginning of that range") comes from the same Chainlink TWAP family, not a contract-set strike. — [dev.to abrownfox001](https://dev.to/abrownfox001/polymarket-twap60-vs-kalshi-why-settlement-design-decides-your-bot-series-14-4gl3)

**Fees (international):**
- Polymarket started charging taker-only fees on 15-minute crypto markets in **January 2026**. The fees fund daily maker rebates. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/384461/polymarket-adds-taker-fees-to-15-minute-crypto-markets-to-fund-liquidity-rebates); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/polymarket-quietly-adds-taker-fees-15-minute-crypto-markets)
- The current docs give `fee = C × feeRate × p × (1 − p)`, with **Crypto taker rate 0.07** and maker rate 0. Makers are "never charged fees." On 100 shares the fee is $1.75 at 50¢, $1.47 at 30¢/70¢, $0.63 at 10¢/90¢ and $0.07 at 1¢/99¢. Fees are rounded to 5 decimals. — [Polymarket docs: Fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees)
- The **maker rebate pool for Crypto is 20%** of taker fees. It is distributed daily in pUSD, weighted by each maker's fee-curve share per market, with a $1 minimum payout. There is also a separate tiered "Taker Rebate Program." — [Polymarket docs: Maker Rebates Program](https://docs.polymarket.com/developers/market-makers/maker-rebates-program)
- Fees now apply to most categories (Sports 0.05, Politics 0.04, etc.), not only 15m crypto. Geopolitics is fee-free. — [Polymarket docs: Fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees)

**Liquidity (international):**
- I sampled 24 BTC 15m windows (one per hour, Oct 10 22:45 ET to Oct 11 21:45 ET, a weekend) through the Gamma API. Per-window volume ranged from about **$8.7k to $158k**, and most windows were $14k–$60k. Displayed order-book liquidity for a freshly opened window was about $5.7k, with a 51/52¢ book and 1¢ tick. Minimum order size is 5 shares. — [Gamma API](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=btc-updown-15m-1791770400)
- Spot checks: Jan 15, 2026 window had $197k volume, Mar 31 had $71k, and Apr 30 to Jul 25 windows had $18k–$51k. — Gamma API (same method)

**US access:**
- International BTC 15m markets carry `restricted: true`. US residents must use the separate Polymarket US app. The two services do not share accounts. — [Gamma API](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=btc-updown-15m-1791770400); [Arabian Post, Sep 28 2026](https://thearabianpost.com/polymarket-adds-15-minute-bitcoin-markets-to-us-app/)
- **Polymarket US** (QCX LLC, CFTC DCM designated July 9, 2025) launched "BTC Up or Down: 15 min." Its rules use **CF Benchmarks BRTI: "60 BRTI observations are collected during the final minute before the specified time and averaged," rounded to 2 dp, for both the opening and closing reference**. Up wins if close ≥ open. — [Arabian Post, Sep 28 2026](https://thearabianpost.com/polymarket-adds-15-minute-bitcoin-markets-to-us-app/)
- Other outlets say the Polymarket US product uses Chainlink. — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/polymarket-15-minute-bitcoin-markets-us/); [next.io](https://next.io/prediction-markets/crypto/binance/15-minute/). These likely conflate it with the international product. A GitHub PR reports that all 24 windows on 2026-09-28 matched Kalshi KXBTC15M exactly on price to beat, settlement value and outcome, which supports BRTI. — [albatrossbird/housedge PR #171](https://github.com/albatrossbird/housedge/pull/171)
- Polymarket US volume: **$91.76M in BTC 15m during its first full week (Sept 23–29, 2026)**, rising from $2.40M per day to $27.71M per day. Median per-market volume rose from about $23.7k to about $182k. — [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/polymarket-us-15-minute-bitcoin-volume-hits-92m-in-first-full-week/)

**Kalshi comparison:**
- KXBTC15M settles on a 60-second simple average of CF Benchmarks BRTI, sampled each second over the final minute. It is the same index family as the other Kalshi BTC frequencies. The claim that it uses "BRRNY" is wrong, since BRRNY is a once-daily fixing. — [PredictionMarketsPicks](https://predictionmarketspicks.com/articles/kalshi-bitcoin-15-minute-markets); [How Kalshi settles bitcoin](https://predictionmarketspicks.com/articles/how-kalshi-settles-bitcoin)
- Kalshi BTC 15m volume: about $226k in its first full week (late 2025). By Sept 1–28, 2026 it was about $7B of Kalshi's $8.17B crypto volume (86%). — [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/polymarket-us-15-minute-bitcoin-volume-hits-92m-in-first-full-week/)
- Kalshi fee as implemented in one arb bot: `ceil(0.07 × C × P × (1−P))`, rounded up to the cent. — [NotACheeto/BTC15ARB](https://github.com/NotACheeto/BTC15ARB) (secondary; check against Kalshi's fee schedule)

### Inferences
- **Kalshi vs Polymarket US is the clean cross-venue pair.** Both use BRTI with a 60s final-minute average and the same ≥ tie rule. Residual risk is mostly execution, fees and rounding of the open reference: Polymarket US averages the minute before the open, while Kalshi sets the strike/reference from its contract terms. Verify those are identical per window.
- **Kalshi vs Polymarket international carries settlement basis risk.** Chainlink TWAP60 and BRTI 60s average are different indices and can disagree on near-zero moves. A "YES Kalshi + NO Poly < $1" position is therefore not riskless. Basis risk is largest when |close − open| is a few dollars, which is exactly when 15m books sit near 50¢. Polymarket international is also not legally accessible to US persons.
- **Pre-Aug-7-2026 data is stale for Polymarket international.** Snapshot settlement no longer applies, so older backtests of sniping or latency on Polymarket's international venue do not carry over.

### Gaps
- I could not get Polymarket US's fee schedule for 15m BTC from a primary source. BTC15ARB assumes a coefficient of 0.0695, which is unverified.
- I could not get the Polymarket US rulebook directly. BRTI settlement rests on the Arabian Post plus one GitHub PR, and other outlets contradict it.
- There is no official Polymarket changelog citation for the TWAP switch date. It is inferred from the API plus press.
- Kalshi's current maker-fee treatment on KXBTC15M was not verified in this pass.

## 2. Documented Kalshi vs Polymarket arbitrage on the same 15-minute window

### Takeaway
Several open-source repos implement Kalshi to Polymarket (or Polymarket US) BTC arbitrage. **None publishes verifiable P&L, opportunity frequency or mispricing size.** The best-documented public effort is a recorder that confirms settlement identity between Kalshi and Polymarket US. I found no independent study measuring how often cross-venue YES+NO < $1 occurs on 15m BTC.

### Cited Findings
- **CarlosIbCu/polymarket-kalshi-btc-arbitrage-bot** (about 253 stars) targets **hourly** BTC markets, not 15m. It checks Poly Down + Kalshi Yes or Poly Up + Kalshi No depending on which strike is higher, and calls sub-$1 sums "risk-free." It does not model fees, fills, latency or resolution-source mismatch, and it reports no results. — [GitHub](https://github.com/CarlosIbCu/polymarket-kalshi-btc-arbitrage-bot)
- **NotACheeto/BTC15ARB** is a "Production Polymarket US & Kalshi BTC 15-Minute Arbitrage Bot." Net edge = $1 − (YES ask + NO ask) − fees − slippage − latency haircut − unwind risk − reserve. Both legs are sent concurrently as IOC. A one-sided fill leads to a cancel, hedge, or emergency close. It requires an exact timestamp match and BRTI 60s on both venues. It has 2 commits, 0 stars, **no live results**, and the frequency or size of opportunities is not reported. — [GitHub](https://github.com/NotACheeto/BTC15ARB)
- **albatrossbird/housedge PR #171** (merged Sep 29, 2026) records Polymarket US 15m BTC books next to Kalshi. On 24 of 24 windows on 2026-09-28, price to beat, settlement and outcome all matched. It notes the Polymarket US markets "appear in no listing," so slugs must be built from the clock. No price-gap statistics were published. — [GitHub PR](https://github.com/albatrossbird/housedge/pull/171)
- Other Kalshi/Polymarket arb repos exist (ImMike/polymarket-arbitrage scans more than 10,000 markets; a Rust/Python "Kalshi-Polymarket" bot). They are not BTC-15m specific and have no published results. — [ImMike/polymarket-arbitrage](https://github.com/ImMike/polymarket-arbitrage); [search result](https://www.sourcepulse.org/projects/25028206)
- A Reddit post on a Kalshi–Polymarket bot warns of "execution risk (it's NOT risk free)." — [Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qebxud/snapshots/2026-01-19T19%3A11%3A34.79073Z) (seen only as a search snippet)
- A developer comparing the venues argues that training on Polymarket TWAP and firing on Kalshi is "transferring a correlation, not a settlement identity." — [dev.to abrownfox001](https://dev.to/abrownfox001/polymarket-twap60-vs-kalshi-why-settlement-design-decides-your-bot-series-14-4gl3)

### Inferences
- **Fee hurdle near 50¢ (Kalshi + Polymarket international):** 0.07×0.25 = 1.75¢ per contract on each leg, or about 3.5¢ total. Kalshi rounds up per order. The asks must therefore sum to about ≤ $0.965 before slippage, which is far wider than the 1¢ spreads seen on Polymarket international books. At extreme prices (e.g. 10¢/90¢) fees fall to about 0.63¢ per leg, so the hurdle is lower but liquidity is thinner. Calculation is from the [Polymarket fee docs](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees) and the Kalshi formula as given in [BTC15ARB](https://github.com/NotACheeto/BTC15ARB).
- **Polymarket US's huge launch volume** (≈400× Kalshi's first week) points to retail-heavy flow on a new venue. That is plausibly where transient cross-venue gaps with Kalshi would be largest, but nobody has published measurements.

### Gaps
- No public data on the frequency, size or duration of Kalshi vs Polymarket (international or US) 15m BTC mispricings.
- No verified P&L from any cross-venue 15m BTC arbitrage bot.

## 3. Pricing or hedging instruments: Deribit options, Kalshi hourly/daily brackets, CME, Binance perps

### Takeaway
No listed option expires in 15 minutes, so an options-implied fair value does not apply directly to KXBTC15M. It does work for Kalshi hourly and daily brackets, where at least one public tool compares an options chain against Kalshi. I found no published evidence of systematic mispricing between Kalshi 15m and Deribit, CME or Binance. Within-window pricing is dominated by spot moves and realized vol.

### Cited Findings
- "No listed option is fifteen minutes to expiry, so the options-implied model… has nothing to compare against… The edge, if any, is microstructure — short-window realized volatility, order-flow imbalance, and the spread/fee drag." The same site runs an options-implied edge tool for Kalshi **hourly** BTC. — [PredictionMarketsPicks](https://predictionmarketspicks.com/articles/kalshi-bitcoin-15-minute-markets)
- Near the start of a 15m window, drift is negligible and price sits near 50¢. The price is "mostly a statement about which way the last few minutes of order flow are leaning, plus a few cents of spread." — [PredictionMarketsPicks](https://predictionmarketspicks.com/articles/kalshi-bitcoin-15-minute-markets)
- Once BTC has clearly moved in a window, Polymarket reprices fast. One bot log shows BTC +0.517% with UP already at $0.99. — [dev.to manja316, Mar 14 2026](https://dev.to/manja316/176-trades-on-polymarket-what-my-bot-actually-made-its-not-what-you-think-3iib)
- Chainlink's TWAP is built from external spot exchanges (Binance, Coinbase, etc.) and relayed over several hops. A bot reading spot faster can anticipate where the displayed TWAP is heading. — [Casatrick Substack](https://casatrick.substack.com/p/polymarket-twap-latency-trading-bots)
- Before TWAP, Polymarket 5m contracts resolved on the same side as Binance about 85% of the time (Bloomberg, via search summary). — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/polymarket-twap-integration-crypto-markets/) (snippet only; not independently verified)

### Inferences
- For KXBTC15M, the useful "other instruments" are **real-time spot feeds** (Coinbase, Kraken, Bitstamp and the other BRTI constituents, plus Binance perps as a lead indicator) used to estimate the running 60s BRTI average, not options. Deribit short-dated IV is useful only as a volatility input for an up/down probability model (e.g. P(up) ≈ Φ(ln(S/K)/(σ√τ))), not as a direct arb leg.
- Hedging a KXBTC15M position with a Binance perp or CME micro future is mechanically possible but only partial. A binary payoff around the strike is mostly gamma risk and cannot be hedged with a linear instrument.

### Gaps
- No public study of KXBTC15M vs Deribit-implied probabilities, or vs KXBTC/KXBTCD bracket-implied distributions.
- No evidence found of cross-instrument arb between KXBTC15M and Kalshi hourly brackets (KXBTCD) at overlapping expiries. This looks worth testing, since an hourly expiry coincides with the :00 15m expiry, but nothing was found.

## 4. Public bot write-ups: edges used, claimed P&L, decay

### Takeaway
There are three well-documented edge types on Polymarket 15m and 5m crypto:
- **Latency arbitrage versus Binance/Coinbase spot.** The famous "0x8dxd" $313 to ~$414–438k claim, from Dec 2025 to Jan 2026, is unverified secondary reporting.
- **Settlement-window manipulation on 5m snapshot markets.** This is academically documented: 821 wallets made $8.22M. It was the trigger for the TWAP switch.
- **Intra-venue "buy both sides < $1" arbitrage.** It is small and real, but fill risk turns it into directional losses.

The 2026 taker fees (January) and TWAP settlement (August 7) were aimed squarely at eroding the first two. Honest first-hand reports show tiny or negative P&L.

### Cited Findings
**Latency / directional bots (pre-fees, pre-TWAP):**
- Wallet **0x8dxd** reportedly started in Dec 2025 with $313 and reached about $437.6k profit by Jan 6, 2026, with a 98% win rate. A Dexter's Lab analyst puts it at about $414k in one month. The strategy is described as watching Binance and Coinbase BTC spot and buying on Polymarket when spot already implies the outcome (~85%) but odds still sit near 50/50. **This is secondary, unaudited reporting, and the figures conflict.** — [MEXC news (Finbold syndication)](https://www.mexc.com/news/417346); [Yahoo Finance: "Arbitrage Bots Dominate Polymarket…"](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/arbitrage-bots-dominate-polymarket-millions-100000888.html) (content seen only via search summary; the fetch failed)
- Claims that "bots earn $5–10k daily" are unverified. — [Phemex](https://phemex.com/news/article/trading-bots-generate-510k-daily-on-polymarket-with-bitcoin-options-52347)
- One developer writing about 5m BTC markets says Polymarket MMs are not colocated with Binance and their quotes lag by 30–90s on large moves. — [IndieHackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-polymarket-arbitrage-bot-exploiting-inefficiencies-between-5-minute-and-15-minute-crypto-markets-a6b2af442f) (via search summary)
- Opportunities "are rare and quickly disappear as competition increases" (search summary of coverage). — [MEXC](https://www.mexc.com/news/417346)

**Settlement manipulation (5m, snapshot era):**
- Stanford/SMU paper "Settlement Manipulation in Prediction Markets": 5m BTC contracts launched Feb 12, 2026, with Chainlink BTC/USD snapshot settlement. It analysed more than 16,000 contracts and more than 60M on-chain records. Directional spot flow in the final 10s rose about 50% after launch and reverted within the following 10s. 1,613 cycles were flagged. **821 wallets (0.34% of ~243k) earned $8.22M in flagged cycles vs $90k in others; retail/unclassified lost $7.61M.** 56% of episodes fell in UTC night hours and 44% on weekends. Intent is not proven. — [ForkLog, Jul 16 2026](https://forklog.com/en/news/researchers-find-signs-of-manipulation-in-polymarket-five-minute-bitcoin-contracts)
- Polymarket responded with Chainlink TWAP settlement from Aug 7, 2026. Commentators say this kills last-tick sniping but not latency edges. Bots can still front-run the oracle pipeline (exchange → Chainlink → RTDS → Polymarket), and book-execution latency is unaffected. — [Casatrick Substack](https://casatrick.substack.com/p/polymarket-twap-latency-trading-bots); [Casatrick "half fixed"](https://casatrick.substack.com/p/polymarket-twap-half-fixed) (title only)

**First-hand small-scale results (Polymarket international 15m, ~Mar 2026, after fees started):**
- 176 resolved trades produced **~$2 net**. Results by strategy:
  - "Buy both sides < $1" arb won on ~97% of 98 fully filled pairs, at $0.10–$0.30 per trade.
  - Late momentum (minutes 8–12, Binance move > 0.20%) was net negative despite a 95.7% backtest win rate.
  - Late snipe (minute 13, buying at $0.93–0.97) was net negative.
  - **Half-fills** turned arbs into naked bets, e.g. two -$20.75 losses in one ETH/XRP window. ETH and XRP were cut as net losers.

  — [dev.to manja316, Mar 14 2026](https://dev.to/manja316/176-trades-on-polymarket-what-my-bot-actually-made-its-not-what-you-think-3iib)

**Kalshi-side bots:**
- seanrobenalt/kalshi-bot (Rust) buys YES+NO when the combined ask is < $1 on 15m crypto. — [GitHub](https://github.com/seanrobenalt/kalshi-bot) (search snippet)
- DeweyMarco simple-kalshi-bot targets KXBTC15M with an ARBITRAGE strategy among others. — [Mintlify docs](https://www.mintlify.com/DeweyMarco/simple-kalshi-bot/strategies/overview) (search snippet)
- brandononchain/kalshibot is a multi-strategy system for BTC binaries. — [GitHub](https://github.com/brandononchain/kalshibot) (search snippet; no results reviewed)

**Commercial and marketing repos (treat as noise):**
- Several repos are SEO-spammed or sold (e.g. PredictiveFlow-Labs, PolyBullLabs, a $499 "polymtradebot"). aulekator's bot cites a ~75% win rate from simulation. — [aulekator](https://github.com/aulekator/Polymarket-BTC-15-Minute-Trading-Bot); [polymtradebot.com](https://polymtradebot.com/)

### Inferences
- The edge decayed in three steps:
  1. Fee-free snapshot markets with slow MMs (late 2025): big latency profits were plausible.
  2. Jan 2026 taker fees: up to 1.75¢ per share at 50¢ eats thin latency and arb edges, and makers get rebates.
  3. Aug 7, 2026 TWAP settlement: last-tick sniping and manipulation are removed.
- The remaining edges are (a) **market making for maker rebates** with a fast spot-informed fair value, and (b) **forecasting the running 60s average** from faster spot feeds.
- Kalshi KXBTC15M already used a 60s BRTI average, so last-second manipulation stories mostly do not transfer to it.

### Gaps
- There is no on-chain verification of the 0x8dxd figures in this pass. A Polymarket profile and on-chain query would be needed.
- There is no first-hand P&L from a Kalshi KXBTC15M bot.
- There is no data on post-TWAP (Aug–Oct 2026) bot profitability.

## 5. "YES + NO asks < $1" (intra-venue) and cross-venue YES(Kalshi) + NO(Poly) < $1: frequency and size after fees

### Takeaway
On Polymarket 15m, intra-venue sub-$1 pairs exist but yield only ~$0.10–$0.30 per trade at small size, with serious leg risk. After the 2026 taker fees, a pair at 49¢+49¢ (2¢ gross) is **negative** for a taker: about 3.5¢ in fees on the two legs. No source quantifies how often cross-venue sub-$1 sums occur on 15m BTC.

### Cited Findings
- Example arb: UP 0.49 + DOWN 0.49 = 0.98 for "guaranteed $0.02" (pre-fee framing). The realized result was 98 fully filled pairs at ~97% win, $0.10–$0.30 each. FOK half-fills caused large losses. — [dev.to manja316](https://dev.to/manja316/176-trades-on-polymarket-what-my-bot-actually-made-its-not-what-you-think-3iib)
- The Polymarket crypto taker fee at p=0.49 is about 0.07×0.49×0.51 ≈ 1.75¢ per share per leg. Taking both legs costs about 3.5¢, which exceeds a 2¢ gross gap. — [Polymarket docs: Fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees)
- Academic baseline (all Polymarket markets, not 15m crypto): about **$40M** of realized arbitrage profit, split between "market rebalancing" (YES+NO ≠ 1) and combinatorial arbitrage, measured over the paper's sample period (≈2024–2025). — [Saguillo et al., "Unravelling the Probabilistic Forest," arXiv 2508.03474 / AFT 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.03474v1)
- BTC15ARB caps unwind loss at $0.02 on a one-sided fill and deducts latency and unwind haircuts before trading. This implies the authors expect frequent leg failures. — [GitHub](https://github.com/NotACheeto/BTC15ARB)

### Inferences
- Intra-venue sub-$1 arb on Polymarket now only pays if at least one leg is filled as a **maker** (zero fee plus rebate), or if prices are far from 50¢ where the fee is small. Pure taker-taker pairs need gaps of ≥ ~3.5¢ near 50¢.
- For Kalshi + Polymarket US, both venues are on BRTI, so a cross-venue sub-$1 sum is close to a true arbitrage. Rough break-even is a 3.5¢ gross gap near 50¢, assuming a Kalshi coefficient of 0.07 and a Polymarket US coefficient of ~0.07 (unverified). Capital is locked on two venues for ≤15 min, and leg risk remains.

### Gaps
- No measured frequency or size distribution of sub-$1 sums (intra-venue or cross-venue) for 15m BTC in any public source. Answering this needs one's own order-book recording on Kalshi, Polymarket international and Polymarket US. The housedge PR shows such recording is feasible.
